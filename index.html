Dylan Jensen - Audio Production and Electric Bass

Dylan Jensen is a true audio production everyman; from mixing and mastering to live sound to studio recording, Dylan does it all.
Add in his live and studio electric bass work, and you’ve got a force to be reckoned with.

Jensen has played electric bass in multiple bands, including Aurora, with whom he played at New Haven’s Shoreline Summer Festival. More recently, Jensen has played with the indie rock duo Not Even Three, for studio and gigs. He appears on their single “Familiar Views”, released in August of 2026, and subsequently released on their album Something to See (October 2026).

Dylan has a few projects on the horizon as he finishes his Audio and Music Production degree at Western Connecticut State University. He is looking to further his skills as a studio tech and mixing engineer, and with his knowledge he hopes to take on more projects throughout 2026 and 2027.
